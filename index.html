02 — How you learn

You will build things.
Then you will be assessed on them.

The programme runs on activity-based and project-based learning alongside the theory. Core theory comes first, taught by instructors — then you apply it, in laboratories, on real problems, with guidance.

MU AI Center · ICT Building · Salaya

03 — A route that leads home

Germany is already on
the destination list.

The faculty advertises internship-abroad rounds every year. Germany appeared in both the 2025 and the 2026 round — alongside Japan, Taiwan, South Korea, Malaysia, Australia and China.

You study in Thailand, in a region investing heavily in cloud services, data centres, artificial intelligence and cybersecurity. You keep a route back into the German market — through an internship, a Master's, or the network you build while you are here.

04 — Straight answers

Questions German students
actually ask us.

For international students the tuition is 90,000 THB per semester — 180,000 THB per academic year, and 720,000 THB across the eight semesters of the degree. A summer session, if one is needed, is 25,000 THB. Fees are set in Thai baht, so check the current exchange rate and confirm the figure on the official fee page before you budget. Accommodation and living costs are separate.
